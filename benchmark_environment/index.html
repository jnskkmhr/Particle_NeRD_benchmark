1. Simulation conditions and recording

Each clip is a fresh native implicit-MPM run. Particles and body states are saved at 200 Hz; video frames are captured from those same states at 50 fps. The first second prepares the object scenes before intrusion. Robot controllers run from reset.

The sand uses density 1,600 kg/m³, Young’s modulus 10¹⁵ Pa, Poisson’s ratio 0.3, material friction 0.48, and yield pressure 10¹⁵ Pa. The MPM grid spacing is 0.04 m. Initial particle positions use a 0.032 m lattice with uniform jitter of up to 6.4 mm per coordinate (seed 42), bounded by the initial sand surface and rigid tray. Particle count and mass are unchanged. Franka’s walls end 4 cm above its initial sand surface; other tray walls end at z=0.20 m. These are simulation parameters, not a calibration to a measured sand specimen.

Validation checks particle containment, finite recorded states and forces, actual material contact, and video timestamps. Robot cases also check upright posture and forward motion. These reference rollouts do not measure Particle NeRD prediction accuracy or GPU scalability.
