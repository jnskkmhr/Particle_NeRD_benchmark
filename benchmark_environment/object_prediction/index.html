2. Held-out recurrent rollouts

Each value first averages time within one trajectory, then weights trajectories equally. ± denotes sample standard deviation across trajectory summaries; n is the number with an available measurement. Failed rollouts contribute their available duration and remain in the denominator of completion counts. No smoothing, alignment, or time shifting is applied.

Across the 60 test trajectories, the unchanged-initial-position baseline has a time-averaged particle-position RMSE of 19.96 ± 8.12 (n=60) mm. Particle NeRD's error at the final six-second frame is 33.22 ± 18.9 (n=60) mm. The table below reports errors averaged over time, which are smaller than final-frame errors when predictions drift.
